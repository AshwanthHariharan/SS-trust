Our Story

Founded in memory of a father of education.

Kesingan SS Trust was founded in 2008 as a closely held trust by Mrs. Saraswathi Swamithan, along with her siblings, in loving memory of Late S. Swaminathan, MA, BEd, Retd. HM, Manalmedu, Mayiladuthurai district.

Late Mr. S. Swaminathan dedicated his career to education management, touching numerous lives through his coaching. A pioneer in education empowerment for women, he ensured all his daughters completed their studies, setting a remarkable example.

As a tribute to this father of education, his siblings formed the trust to empower underprivileged students through free coaching in Kesingan village, his birthplace. Over the past 16 years, with God's grace, around 80 students have benefited annually, transforming families' lives. The Kesingan panchayat school is now rated among the best in the district, with the Headmaster acknowledging the trust's significant contribution.
